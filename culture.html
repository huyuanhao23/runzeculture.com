Our story

A Mong Kok office. A global outlook.

Runzeculture started as a small consultancy on the upper floors of Lucky Horse Industrial Building in Mong Kok. Our founders were a cultural economist returning from London, a producer coming off a touring exhibition, and a software engineer who’d shipped a ticketing app for the West Kowloon Cultural District.

They saw the same gap over and over: brilliant cultural work, and second-rate operational infrastructure. So they built a practice that pairs editorial taste with operational rigour — and a mobile app that puts every project update into one inbox.

Eight years later, Runzeculture operates from Mong Kok with project desks in London and New York. We now offer eleven services, ship a culture-manager app on both Google Play and the App Store, and partner with over a hundred creators, institutions, and brands every year.

Social responsibility

Two commitments we’ve made openly.

  1. 1. Cultural education

    5% of senior-team hours every quarter are reserved for pro-bono consulting with cultural-education non-profits in HK and SEA. Mentorship + workshops, no marketing strings attached.

  2. 2. Sustainable supply chain

    Every arts & crafts sourcing partner is asked to disclose material origin and working conditions. We publish an annual supply-chain report and work only with suppliers who align with our standards.
