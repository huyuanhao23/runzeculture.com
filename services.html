Cultural Project Investment(文化項目投資)

Sourcing, due diligence, and co-investment support for cross-border cultural projects — from exhibitions and festivals to IP-rich creative ventures. We structure deals with HK, mainland, and international partners.

Artistic Activity Planning(文藝活動策劃)

Full-service planning for art events — concept, production, talent, venue, ticketing, and visitor experience. We deliver gallery openings, festivals, public-art activations, and pop-up cultural programmes.

Film & TV Content Planning(影視內容策劃)

Development support for film, scripted, documentary, and short-form content — greenlight decks, location scouting, talent attachment, festival strategy, and cross-border co-production.

Brand Planning & Promotion(品牌策劃推廣)

Brand strategy and integrated promotion for cultural, lifestyle, and creator-led businesses expanding across HK, mainland China, SEA, the US, UK, and the EU.

Exhibition & Display Services(展覽展示服務)

End-to-end exhibition delivery — design, fabrication, shipping, on-site installation, and visitor analytics. Specialisation in touring cultural exhibitions across multiple jurisdictions.

Cultural & Creative Products(文創產品)

Concept-to-shelf product development for museums, brands, and cultural institutions — limited-edition merchandise, museum shops, and IP-licensed goods.

Arts & Crafts(工藝美術品)

Sourcing, curation, and trade in arts & crafts. We work with master craftspeople, ateliers, and creative studios across Asia and Europe with full export documentation support.

Publication-Related Consulting(出版物相關諮詢)

Editorial strategy, rights management, and cross-border publishing consulting — books, catalogues, magazines, and digital-first publications for cultural organisations and independent creators.

Business Consulting(商務顧問)

Strategic advisory for creative businesses — commercial model design, partnership structuring, M&A support, and investor relations. Bilingual senior team across HK and London.

Online Marketing(網絡營銷)

Performance marketing, content strategy, and creator-led campaigns. We deploy and operate campaigns on Google, Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and WeChat with analytics and reporting baked in.

Import & Export of Goods and Technology(貨物及技術進出口)

Cross-border trade support for cultural and creative goods — customs documentation, logistics, HS classification, IP licensing, and dual-jurisdiction compliance (HK & mainland China).

How we work

A four-step engagement model.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A 60-minute bilingual intake call with our senior team — context, objectives, timelines, and constraints documented in your private project workspace.

  2. 02

    Scoping

    A written scope, fixed budget envelope, key deliverables, risk register, and partner intro decks. Turn-around: 5 business days for most engagements.

  3. 03

    Execution

    A weekly status review with your dedicated lead plus shared dashboards in our mobile app — bookable on-site or remote across HK, London, and New York.

  4. 04

    Reporting

    A closing report with financials, learnings, follow-on roadmap, and introductions to partner firms where relevant. 30-day post-engagement support is included.
