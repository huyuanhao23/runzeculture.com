Runzeculture: Culture Manager

Eleven services. One pocket-sized studio.

The mobile culture manager app mirrors every service line on runzeculture.com — investment dashboards, event planners, production boards, brand campaign trackers, exhibition timelines, product catalogues, maker galleries, publishing intake, business-booking, marketing analytics, and trade documentation tools — all in a single Android & iOS app.

  • Per-business-line dashboards with bilingual UI (EN + ZH).
  • Push notifications for project updates & deadlines.
  • Document upload, e-sign, and in-app purchase for premium tiers.
  • Consent-driven ads with UMP + ATT; full privacy controls.

Our story

A Mong Kok office. A global outlook.

Runzeculture was built on a simple idea — that cultural work deserves the same operational rigour as any global industry. From our office in Mong Kok, Hong Kong, we staff eleven business lines that support creators, brands, and institutions across the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and the wider APAC region.

Our team is deliberately small, deliberately senior, and deliberately global. Every engagement is anchored in craft, candor, and cross-border fluency.
